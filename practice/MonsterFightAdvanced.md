# Monster Fight Second Edition

Let's improve your previous Monster brawl simulation! Instead of just one monster on each side, we will have an entire party of monsters on each side! Monsters on each side take turns attacking each other, one at a time

## Requirements
- You will make a copy of your previous Monster brawl simulation to modify
- Ask the user how many Monsters to input on the allied side and opposing side and allow them to input that many Monsters
- Monsters on each side will only attack one Monster at a time
    - The first alive Monster on the attacking side will attack the first living Monster on the other side
- Play these turns and tell the user which side won at the end - the amount of monsters left will be a tiebreaker. The second tiebreaker will be the hp of the first living Monster on each side
